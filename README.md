## SIH 26033 — HarvestPulse
### Demand-Driven Farm-to-Market Network

## Proposed Solution
### Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution

HarvestPulse is a digital marketplace connecting farmers, FPOs, consumers, and bulk buyers directly. Its unique feature, Demand-First Harvest Pooling, combines nearby farmers' produce to fulfil buyer orders efficiently.

### How It Addresses the Problem

The platform reduces intermediaries, improves price transparency, forecasts demand, and coordinates shared transportation to help reduce costs and prevent unsold produce.

### Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution

HarvestPulse combines demand forecasting, shared harvest lots, transparent pricing, and route optimization. Its Demand Gap Alert identifies shortages and excess supply to support better planning.

## Technical Approach

### Technologies to be used 

- Frontend: React.js, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

- Backend: Python with FastAPI

- Database: PostgreSQL

- Demand Forecasting: Python and Scikit-learn

- Route Optimization: OpenStreetMap and OSRM

### Methodology and Process for Implementation

1. Register farmers and buyers and collect crop listings and orders.

2. Forecast demand using historical sales and current orders.

3. Match supply with demand and create shared harvest lots.

4. Calculate costs and optimize collection and delivery routes.

5. Track deliveries, record payments, and update sales data.

## Feasibility and Viability

### Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea

The platform can be developed using existing web technologies, databases, forecasting libraries, and mapping services. A small prototype can begin with sample data and a limited group of farmers and buyers.

### Potential Challenges and Risks

- Unpredictable supply and demand.

- Differences in produce quality.

- Limited digital literacy and connectivity.

- Transportation costs and order cancellations.

- Insufficient historical data for accurate forecasting.

### Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges

Use local-language support, quality checks, short-term forecasting, confirmed orders, transparent cancellation policies, and shared deliveries where cost-effective.

## Impact and Benefits

### Potential Impact on the Target Audience

Farmers gain direct market access and better price visibility. Consumers and bulk buyers gain easier procurement, while FPOs benefit from improved supply coordination.

### Benefits of the Solution

- Economic: Potentially better farmer earnings and lower transaction costs.

- Social: Improved access to digital markets.

- Environmental: Potentially fewer unnecessary transport trips and less food waste.

- Operational: More efficient procurement, collection, and delivery.
